## Independent example / 独立計算

Approved blue filer with complete accrual double-entry books and both required statements, transmitted by e-Tax within the deadline. No social or other eligible personal deductions except the basic allowance; no withholding and no advance tax. The fixture deliberately uses zeros for test isolation.

1. ¥3,000,000 receipts − ¥600,000 expenses = ¥2,400,000 pre-blue profit. The journal debits and credits are both ¥3,600,000. The E2E test makes a separate ¥10,000 bank-to-cash adjustment; both sides then total ¥3,610,000 without changing profit.
2. ¥2,400,000 − ¥650,000 − ¥1,040,000 = ¥710,000 taxable income.
3. ¥710,000 × 5% = ¥35,500; ¥35,500 × 2.1% = ¥745.5 → ¥745.
4. ¥36,245 → **¥36,200 payable**. These are national income and reconstruction taxes only.
